# Gemini 3.6 Flash: Google's Fastest Frontier Model

## Model Overview
**Gemini 3.6 Flash** is Google DeepMind's fastest frontier-class model, released on **July 21, 2026** alongside Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber. It is ranked **#1 fastest model** among 186 models tested by Artificial Analysis at **275.5 output tokens per second**, while scoring **50** on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — well above the 31-point average for comparable models. With native multimodal input (text, image, audio, video), a **1M token context window**, and built-in hybrid reasoning, Gemini 3.6 Flash occupies the sweet spot between frontier intelligence and deployment speed.
