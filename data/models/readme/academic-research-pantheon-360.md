## ✨ Key Features

- 3D-aware 360° video diffusion from single or sparse panoramic images
- Explicit 3D Cache acting as a geometric scaffold for camera trajectory control
- Eliminates temporal drift and cross-view inconsistency inherent in narrow-FoV video generators
- First video diffusion model supporting seamless 360° video interpolation and long-horizon synthesis
- Applications in digital twin creation, camera motion stabilization, and Street View enhancement
