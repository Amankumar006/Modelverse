# Claude Opus 4.6

## Model Overview
Released on February 5, 2026, Claude Opus 4.6 represents a significant architectural leap in Anthropic's flagship Opus model line. Focusing on deep causal reasoning and agentic workflows, Opus 4.6 introduced native capabilities for coordinating agent teams directly. It is an API-only multimodal model (text, image, code) featuring a massive 1 million token context window. 

*Note: As of mid-2026, this model has been superseded by Claude Opus 4.7, 4.8, and the Claude 5 generation.*

## Capabilities
- **Agent Team Coordination:** Built-in mechanisms to orchestrate multi-agent setups, delegating tasks and managing complex, multi-step execution pipelines natively.
- **Adaptive Thinking:** The ability for the model to dynamically adjust its reasoning compute and effort based on the complexity of the prompt, ensuring efficiency.
- **Deep Causal Reasoning:** Heavy architectural optimizations aimed at improving the model's understanding of cause and effect in complex logic puzzles and strategic planning.
- **Claude in PowerPoint Integrations:** Specialized integrations for generating, formatting, and structuring professional presentations natively.

## Example Use Cases
- **Autonomous Project Management:** Serving as a "manager" agent that reads a complex software requirement and spins up sub-agents to write code, test, and document the project.
- **Corporate Presentations:** Ingesting quarterly earnings data and automatically generating a structured PowerPoint presentation with talking points.
- **Advanced Codebase Navigation:** Using the 1M token context and adaptive thinking to deeply analyze legacy code, mapping out variable states and causal links to find obscure bugs.

## Performance & Benchmarks
Claude Opus 4.6 showed a marked improvement in long-context reasoning and agentic tasks over 4.5. While exact benchmark numbers remain undisclosed, the inclusion of adaptive thinking allowed it to score exceptionally well on hard reasoning and long-horizon planning evaluations, significantly reducing the error rate in multi-step workflows.

## Intended Use & Limitations
**Intended Use:** Aimed at advanced developers building complex agentic networks, enterprise automation, and users needing deep, multi-step logical deduction.
**Limitations:** As a highly capable model, its inference cost and latency (especially when utilizing full adaptive thinking) can be higher than lighter models. Its capabilities have since been eclipsed by Opus 4.7 and 4.8, making it an intermediate step in the 4.x lineage.
