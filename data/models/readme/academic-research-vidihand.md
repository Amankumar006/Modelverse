## ✨ Key Features

- Detector-Free Full-Frame Processing without localized cropping or hand detectors
- Leverages Internet-Scale Pretrained Video Diffusion Models (Wan2.1-VACE)
- Extreme Robustness to Heavy Hand-Object and Hand-Hand Occlusions
- Eliminates Test-Time Optimization (TTO) and post-hoc temporal infilling
- State-of-the-Art Temporal Smoothness (jitter down to 3.18 mm/frame) and Pose Accuracy (21.668 mm MPJPE-p on ARCTIC)
