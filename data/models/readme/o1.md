# o1 (o1)

## Model Overview
OpenAI’s **o1** is a series of AI models specifically engineered for complex reasoning. Departing from standard large language models that generate immediate responses, the o1 series "thinks" through problems step-by-step before answering. The family includes the primary `o1` model, as well as cost-effective variants like `o1-mini` and compute-heavy versions like `o1-pro`. It represents a significant shift toward deliberate, reasoning-based AI systems.

## Capabilities
- **Chain-of-Thought Reasoning:** Utilizes an internal reasoning process to break down multi-step problems, evaluate various solution paths, and self-correct logic before generating an output.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** Trained using reinforcement learning to reward effective problem-solving behavior.
- **Enhanced Safety:** Advanced reasoning abilities contribute to improved resistance against "jailbreaking" and policy-violating prompts.
- **Text Modality:** Primarily focused on text-based complex reasoning.

## Example Use Cases
- **Advanced Mathematics:** Solving intricate mathematical proofs and problems.
- **Software Engineering:** Complex coding tasks, deep debugging, and algorithm development.
- **Scientific Research:** Analyzing complex scientific data and logical problem-solving.
- **Strategic Planning:** Multi-step strategic planning and analysis.

## Performance & Benchmarks
The o1 models excel in domains requiring deep logical analysis, often vastly outperforming standard models like GPT-4o on complex reasoning benchmarks (such as competitive programming or high-level math competitions). The `o1-pro` version utilizes additional compute to achieve even higher complexity reasoning. However, this comes at the cost of higher latency due to the "thinking" time required.

## Intended Use & Limitations
- **Intended Use:** Designed for complex, non-urgent tasks requiring deep thought, accessible via the OpenAI API.
- **Limitations:** o1 has higher latency compared to standard models and is generally not suited for real-time, low-latency applications like simple chatbots. For safety reasons, the raw internal "chain-of-thought" is hidden from the user.

## About OpenAI
OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company dedicated to ensuring that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. They are the creators of the GPT series of language models, DALL-E, and advanced reasoning models like the `o1` and `o3` families.
