# Suno v4: Full-Length AI Music Generation

## Model Overview
**Suno v4** is Suno's flagship generative model for full-length music, vocal, and song production in high-fidelity stereo, released in November 2024. Building on Suno v3, v4 produces complete songs with structured lyrics and vocals across any musical genre with enhanced audio fidelity, a greater dynamic range, and built-in AI lyrics assistance. Suno v4 is widely regarded as the most capable consumer music generation model at its release, enabling anyone to create professional-sounding original songs in seconds.

---

## ✨ Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **Full Song Generation** | Produces complete songs with structured lyrics, melody, harmony, and vocals |
| **Enhanced Fidelity** | Improved stereo audio quality with greater dynamic range vs. v3 |
| **AI Lyrics Assistant** | Built-in AI lyrics assistance for collaborative song creation |
| **Vocal Realism** | Supports realistic vocal tones and styles across all major music genres |
| **Genre Versatility** | Rock, pop, hip-hop, classical, jazz, electronic, and more |
