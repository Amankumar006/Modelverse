# Claude Opus 4.5

## Model Overview
Claude Opus 4.5 is a highly capable, frontier-class artificial intelligence model developed by Anthropic. Officially released on November 24, 2025, this update in the Claude 4.5 family focuses heavily on advanced reasoning, coding, and handling complex enterprise workflows. Designed as an API-only model, it natively handles text, images, and code. One of its most notable introductions was the "Infinite Chats" feature, engineered to gracefully handle context window limitations and maintain continuity over long, intensive sessions.

*Note: While once the flagship, Claude Opus 4.5 has since been succeeded by newer iterations in the Opus lineup and other series such as Fable and Sonnet 5 as of mid-2026.*

## Capabilities
- **Infinite Chats:** A standout implementation allowing users to maintain continuity in long-running tasks despite context window limits.
- **Multimodal Proficiency:** Excellent at processing and correlating information across text, image, and code inputs.
- **Advanced Workplace Generation:** Exceptionally tuned for producing professional spreadsheets and engaging in complex workplace reasoning.
- **Vast Context Window:** Supports up to 1 million tokens, enabling the analysis of entire codebases, large datasets, and extensive document libraries.

## Example Use Cases
- **Enterprise Data Analysis:** Automatically digesting financial reports and outputting structured, highly formatted spreadsheets.
- **Large-Scale Refactoring:** Utilizing the 1M token context to ingest large code repositories, identify bugs, and propose system-wide architectural refactors.
- **Continuous Agentic Workflows:** Using the Infinite Chats feature to act as a persistent autonomous agent, running background research and managing computer-use tasks without losing session context.

## Performance & Benchmarks
At launch, Claude Opus 4.5 was positioned as Anthropic's most powerful frontier model. While specific empirical benchmark scores were undisclosed by Anthropic, it demonstrated top-tier performance across autonomous agent evaluations, complex coding benchmarks (such as HumanEval and SWE-bench), and complex workplace logic tests. 

## Intended Use & Limitations
**Intended Use:** Designed for enterprise developers, data scientists, and power users who require complex reasoning, coding, and data synthesis over massive contexts.
**Limitations:** Like all LLMs, it may still occasionally hallucinate or struggle with highly specialized niche domains. As an older model (succeeded by 4.6, 4.7, 4.8, etc.), it lacks the deeper causal reasoning and agent coordination features introduced in subsequent generations.

## About Anthropic
Anthropic is an AI safety and research company based in San Francisco, known for developing the Claude series of AI models. Anthropic focuses on creating reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. Their Constitutional AI approach aims to train models that are helpful, honest, and harmless.
