## Model Overview

Gemini 1.5 Pro is Google DeepMind's flagship mid-size multimodal model, delivering highly capable performance across a wide range of tasks. Its standout feature is a revolutionary massive context window, allowing it to process and reason over an unprecedented amount of information in a single prompt.

## Capabilities

* **Massive Context Window:** Gemini 1.5 Pro features a standard context window of 1 million tokens, expandable up to 2 million tokens. This allows it to process vast amounts of information simultaneously, such as 2 hours of video, 22 hours of audio, over 60,000 lines of code, or over 1.4 million words.
* **Native Multimodality:** It is built from the ground up to understand and process text, images, audio, and video natively, without relying on separate models for different modalities.
* **In-Context Learning:** With its massive context window, Gemini 1.5 Pro excels at learning new skills directly from information provided in the prompt, reducing the need for fine-tuning.
* **Advanced Reasoning:** It demonstrates significant improvements in complex reasoning tasks, math, and coding compared to its predecessor, Gemini 1.0 Pro.

## Example Use Cases

* **Large-Scale Data Analysis:** Analyzing entire financial reports, legal documents, or entire books in a single query.
* **Video Understanding:** Analyzing long-form videos to extract specific information, summarize plot points, or answer questions about visual details.
* **Codebase Comprehension:** Providing the model with an entire software repository to understand its architecture, find bugs, or suggest improvements.

## Performance & Benchmarks

Gemini 1.5 Pro achieves state-of-the-art performance across numerous benchmarks, often exceeding the performance of larger models:
* **MMLU (General Knowledge):** 81.9%
* **HumanEval (Code):** 84.1%
* **MATH:** 58.5%
* **Needle In A Haystack (Recall):** 99% recall accuracy across its 1 million token context window.
