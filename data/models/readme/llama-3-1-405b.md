## Model Overview

Llama 3.1 405B is Meta's flagship open-weights foundation model. With 405 billion parameters, it is one of the largest and most capable openly available models in the world, rivaling the performance of top closed-source models across a wide range of benchmarks. 

## Capabilities

* **State-of-the-Art Open Weights:** Competes directly with leading proprietary models like GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet in general knowledge, steerability, math, tool use, and multilingual translation.
* **Massive Scale:** Trained on over 15 trillion tokens of data using 16,000 H100 GPUs, giving it unprecedented capacity for reasoning and knowledge retention for an open model.
* **Extended Context:** Supports an expanded context window of 128K tokens, allowing it to process long documents, entire codebases, and extensive chat histories.
* **Ecosystem Enabler:** Its open availability allows developers to use it for synthetic data generation, model distillation, and fine-tuning, empowering a new wave of specialized AI models.

## Example Use Cases

* **Model Distillation:** Using the 405B model's outputs to train smaller, more efficient models (like the 8B and 70B variants) for specific tasks.
* **Synthetic Data Generation:** Creating high-quality datasets for training other AI systems.
* **Enterprise AI:** Deploying powerful AI entirely on-premises for maximum data privacy and security.

## Performance & Benchmarks

Llama 3.1 405B pushes the boundaries of open-source AI:
* **MMLU (General Knowledge):** 88.6%
* **HumanEval (Code):** 89.0%
* **MATH:** 73.8%
* **Multilingual:** Supports 8 languages with high fluency.

## Open Source Philosophy

Meta released Llama 3.1 405B under a permissive license (with a 700 million MAU commercial limit) to democratize access to frontier-level AI. Meta believes that open-sourcing AI models drives innovation, improves safety through community scrutiny, and prevents a monopoly on AI technology.
