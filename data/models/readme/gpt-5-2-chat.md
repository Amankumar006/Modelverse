# GPT-5.2 Chat

## Model Overview
GPT-5.2 Chat is the conversational variant of the GPT-5.2 frontier model, integrated into ChatGPT upon its release on December 11, 2025. It brought the advanced reasoning, multimodal processing, and long-context understanding of the GPT-5.2 family directly to consumer and enterprise chat interfaces. *Note: As of June 12, 2026, GPT-5.2 Chat has been officially retired and is no longer available in ChatGPT, having been superseded by newer models like GPT-5.6.*

## Capabilities
*   **Conversational AI:** Fine-tuned specifically for natural, engaging, and highly coherent conversational interactions.
*   **Adjustable Reasoning:** Supported dynamic reasoning effort, allowing the model to "think" before responding to complex prompts.
*   **Multimodal Processing:** Seamlessly handled text, code, and vision capabilities within the chat interface.
*   **Tool Integration:** Exhibited highly reliable tool-use accuracy, allowing it to interact seamlessly with browsing, code execution, and external integrations.

## Example Use Cases
*   **Everyday Assistance:** Fast, conversational responses for general inquiries using the Instant tier.
*   **Complex Multistep Reasoning:** Working through difficult math problems, logic puzzles, or strategic planning with adjustable thinking effort.
*   **Content Generation:** Drafting high-quality articles, professional emails, and creative writing with deep contextual awareness.

## Performance & Benchmarks
While sharing the core architecture of GPT-5.2, the chat variant excelled in conversational benchmarks and human preference evaluations. It inherited the strong underlying metrics of the base model, including top-tier performance on general logic (ARC-AGI-1) and tool use (Tau2 Telecom).

## Intended Use & Limitations
GPT-5.2 Chat was intended for users seeking an advanced, interactive AI assistant for both daily tasks and professional work. Its primary limitation was the trade-off between response speed and reasoning depth; complex queries required noticeable "thinking" time. Additionally, as a retired model, it is no longer accessible for new use, and users must migrate to newer models in the ChatGPT ecosystem.
