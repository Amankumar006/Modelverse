## Model Overview
**GPT-4.It was designed to focus on scaling unsupervised learning, improving pattern recognition, and enhancing "emotional intelligence" for creative writing and insights, distinguishing it from the reasoning-heavy o-series models. **Note:** GPT-4.5 is a deprecated model. It was removed from the API in July 2025 and completely sunset from ChatGPT on June 27, 2026.

## Capabilities
- **Enhanced Emotional Intelligence:** Tuned for better nuance, empathy, and pattern recognition in text, making it highly effective for creative and conversational tasks.
- **Creative Writing & Insights:** Optimized for generating high-quality written content, brainstorming, and providing creative insights rather than strict logical reasoning.
- **Scaled Unsupervised Learning:** Leveraged advanced scaling laws to improve general language understanding and fluency.

## Example Use Cases
- **Creative Content Generation:** Assisting authors, marketers, and creators in drafting nuanced, engaging, and creative text.
- **Nuanced Conversational AI:** Powering chatbots and virtual assistants that require a higher degree of empathy, tone matching, and emotional intelligence.
- **Brainstorming & Ideation:** Acting as a creative partner to generate diverse ideas, narratives, and conceptual frameworks.

## Performance & Benchmarks
GPT-4.5 excelled in tasks requiring high emotional intelligence and creative generation, though it was not explicitly optimized for the rigorous logical reasoning benchmarks targeted by the o-series models. Specific parameter counts and context window sizes remain undisclosed. 

## Intended Use & Limitations
GPT-4.5 was intended for creative writing, pattern recognition, and conversational applications via ChatGPT Plus/Pro and the OpenAI API. **Critical Limitation:** The model is entirely deprecated and sunset as of June 2026. It is no longer accessible for new deployments, and users must transition to more current models.
