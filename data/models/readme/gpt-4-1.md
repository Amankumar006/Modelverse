## Model Overview
**GPT-4.1** is a family of large language models released by OpenAI on April 14, 2025. It was designed to enhance the performance of previous models like GPT-4o and GPT-4.5, offering superior coding capabilities, a massive context window, and better instruction following. **Note:** As of July 9, 2026, OpenAI has officially retired the GPT-4.1 series from ChatGPT and the API.

## Capabilities
- **Massive Context Window:** Supports a 1 million token context window across all models in the family, a significant increase over the 128K limit of predecessor models.
- **Advanced Coding & Reasoning:** Features substantial gains in software engineering tasks and complex reasoning, scoring high on benchmarks like SWE-bench Verified.
- **Reliable Instruction Following:** Exhibits enhanced reliability in adhering to complex prompts, constraints, and generating structured outputs.
- **Efficient Variants:** Includes GPT-4.1 mini (optimized for efficiency) and GPT-4.1 nano (the fastest, most cost-effective variant for high-throughput tasks).

## Example Use Cases
- **Large-Scale Document Analysis:** Utilizing the 1 million token context window to process and analyze massive legal, financial, or technical documents in a single prompt.
- **Advanced Software Development:** Assisting engineers with complex coding tasks, bug fixing across large codebases, and architectural design using its improved reasoning capabilities.
- **Automated Data Processing:** Relying on its strong instruction-following to reliably generate structured JSON outputs or parse complex datasets in production pipelines.

## Performance & Benchmarks
GPT-4.1 was specifically engineered to improve upon the performance of its predecessors, with notable, significant gains in software engineering benchmarks like SWE-bench Verified. It stood as OpenAI's smartest non-reasoning model at the time of its release, though specific parameter counts remain undisclosed.

## Intended Use & Limitations
Initially intended for complex reasoning, coding, and large-context tasks via the OpenAI API and ChatGPT tiers. **Important Limitation:** The GPT-4.1 series is now retired (as of July 2026) and represents a deprecated generation of OpenAI's models. Users building new applications are encouraged to migrate to newer model families (e.g., the o-series or GPT-5.x).
