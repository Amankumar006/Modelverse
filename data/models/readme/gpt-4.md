# GPT-4

## Model Overview
GPT-4 is a highly capable, large multimodal foundation model developed by OpenAI, representing a significant evolution in artificial intelligence since its initial release in March 2023. Capable of processing both text and image inputs to generate sophisticated, human-like text responses, GPT-4 sets a high standard for natural language processing, complex reasoning, and creativity. While it is an older model compared to its successors like GPT-4o, it remains a robust choice for complex tasks requiring deep logic and reasoning.

## Capabilities
* **Multimodality:** GPT-4 can accept and interpret a mixture of inputs, including text and images, allowing it to perform tasks like explaining visual content, translating text within images, or analyzing complex diagrams.
* **Complex Reasoning:** It demonstrates marked improvements over its predecessors (such as GPT-3.5) in handling nuanced instructions, creative writing, and solving complex logic or reasoning problems.
* **Expanded Context:** The model handles significant context windows to parse and retain context over long documents or intricate multi-turn conversations.

## Example Use Cases
* **Professional & Creative Work:** Coding, debugging, drafting emails, content creation, and summarizing lengthy documents.
* **Business Efficiency:** Customer support automation, sales assistance, and deep data analysis.
* **Technical Integration:** Developers leverage the API to build applications ranging from intelligent chatbots and voice assistants to complex data processing pipelines.

## Performance & Benchmarks
GPT-4 has demonstrated high-level performance on standardized tests, often outperforming many contemporary models upon its release.
* **Academic Performance:** Achieved scores in the 90th percentile on the Uniform Bar Exam and exceptionally high marks on various Olympiads (e.g., 99th percentile in the Biology Olympiad).
* **Broad Knowledge Base:** It leverages a vast repository of pre-training data to answer questions across a multitude of domains with high accuracy.

## Intended Use & Limitations
* **Hallucinations:** Despite significant improvements, GPT-4 is not fully reliable; it can still "hallucinate" facts or make logical reasoning errors. OpenAI advises caution when using outputs in high-stakes contexts.
* **Safety & Bias:** Like all LLMs, GPT-4 may produce biased or offensive content, though it includes safety mitigations like RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback).
* **Knowledge Cutoff:** Its base knowledge is limited to data available up to its training cutoff date, requiring external tools for real-time information retrieval.

## About OpenAI
OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company dedicated to ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity. Founded in 2015, OpenAI has been at the forefront of AI innovation, developing groundbreaking models like the GPT series (GPT-3, GPT-4) and DALL-E, shaping the future of AI applications worldwide.
