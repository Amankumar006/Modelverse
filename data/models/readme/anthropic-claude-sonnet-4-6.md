## Model Overview

Claude Sonnet 4.6 is a highly capable large language model developed by Anthropic, released on February 17, 2026. As a major upgrade to the Sonnet family, this model was purpose-built to handle scaled professional work, particularly focusing on coding workflows and agent tooling. Operating entirely as an API-only model, Claude Sonnet 4.6 supports a massive 1-million-token context window (in beta), allowing it to ingest and process entire codebases, comprehensive contracts, or extensive research documents in a single request.

## Capabilities

Claude Sonnet 4.6 is a multimodal model (text, image, code) optimized for complex, autonomous tasks. Key capabilities include:
- **Agentic Planning and Execution**: Highly optimized for autonomous workflows where the model plans, executes, and iterates on tasks.
- **Computer Use**: Advanced support for computer use, allowing it to interface with tools similarly to a human operator.
- **Massive Context Window**: Ability to reason over up to 1 million tokens, making it uniquely suited for dense information retrieval and long-context reasoning.
- **Coding Workflows**: Exceptional proficiency in software engineering tasks, from writing and debugging code to understanding large, undocumented codebases.

## Example Use Cases

- **Software Engineering**: Autonomously resolving complex bugs, writing extensive new features, or refactoring large legacy codebases.
- **Legal and Financial Analysis**: Reviewing and cross-referencing massive contracts, financial reports, and regulatory filings within a single prompt context.
- **Agentic Automation**: Powering AI agents that can browse the web, use terminal commands, and navigate operating systems to complete multi-step tasks.
- **Research Synthesis**: Summarizing and extracting insights from dozens of academic papers or long-form books simultaneously.

## Performance & Benchmarks

Upon its release, Claude Sonnet 4.6 delivered "near-Opus" performance, providing a significant capability leap over its predecessor, Sonnet 4.5. It achieved state-of-the-art results in long-context retrieval, coding benchmarks (such as SWE-bench), and agentic tool-use evaluations, setting a new standard for mid-tier models before the release of Claude Sonnet 5.

## Intended Use & Limitations

The model is intended for professional, enterprise, and developer use cases requiring high reliability and scale. 
- **Limitations**: While highly capable, it may still hallucinate on extremely niche topics. Furthermore, its massive context window requires careful prompt engineering to ensure the model focuses on the most relevant information without getting lost in the noise. Note that as of June 2026, Sonnet 4.6 has been superseded by Claude Sonnet 5.

## About Anthropic

Anthropic is an AI safety and research company based in San Francisco, founded by former OpenAI members. The company focuses on developing reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. Their flagship Claude family of models is known for its emphasis on safety, constitutional AI principles, and high performance in enterprise applications.
