## Model Overview
Grok-2 is a frontier Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language model developed by xAI. Released in beta on August 13, 2024, it features approximately 270 billion total parameters, activating around 115 billion parameters per token. Grok-2 represents a significant step forward from its predecessors, offering advanced reasoning, coding capabilities, and seamless real-time information retrieval natively integrated with the X (formerly Twitter) platform.

## Capabilities
- **Real-Time Information Access:** Grok-2 natively integrates with X, allowing it to retrieve and synthesize real-time data and news, offering dynamic and current responses.
- **Advanced Reasoning & Math:** The model demonstrates frontier-level improvements in complex reasoning, mathematical problem-solving, and logic.
- **Multimodal Functionality:** Grok-2 supports native image generation, powered through a partnership with Black Forest Labs.
- **Coding & Instruction Following:** Highly capable in software development tasks, debugging, and adhering to strict instructions.
- **Improved Tool Use:** Excels at identifying missing information, reasoning through sequences of events, and filtering out irrelevant content.

## Example Use Cases
- **Real-Time News Analysis:** Synthesizing current events, breaking news, and trending topics on X.
- **Software Engineering:** Assisting developers with writing code, debugging, and architecture design.
- **Content Creation:** Generating high-quality text and images for social media, marketing, or creative projects.
- **Complex Problem Solving:** Tackling advanced mathematical equations or logical reasoning challenges.

## Performance & Benchmarks
At its launch, Grok-2 performed exceptionally well on competitive benchmarks:
- **LMSYS Chatbot Arena:** Tested under the pseudonym "sus-column-r," it outperformed leading models like GPT-4 Turbo and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on the Elo leaderboard.
- **MMLU:** 87.5%
- **MMLU-Pro:** 70.9%
- **MATH-500:** 77.8%
- **HumanEval:** 86.3%
- **GPQA Diamond:** 51.0%
- **LiveCodeBench:** 26.7%

## Intended Use & Limitations
**Intended Use:** Grok-2 is intended for users requiring real-time knowledge, advanced coding assistance, and multimodal generation via the xAI API and X platform. 
**Limitations:** As a closed-source model, its inner workings are proprietary. While it excels at real-time data, it may inherit biases present in social media data from X. Hallucinations and occasional reasoning errors can still occur, typical of large language models.
