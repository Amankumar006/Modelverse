## Model Overview

Gen-3 Alpha is Runway's latest foundational model for video generation. It represents a major step forward in building General World Models, designed to simulate a wide range of situations, interactions, and physics within the real world. 

## Capabilities

* **High-Fidelity Video Generation:** Gen-3 Alpha excels at generating highly realistic video clips with excellent temporal consistency, meaning objects and characters maintain their appearance smoothly across frames.
* **Precise Control:** The model offers advanced control over the generation process, allowing users to specify exact camera movements (panning, tilting, tracking), object trajectories, and lighting changes within their prompts.
* **Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video:** It supports both generating video entirely from text descriptions and animating existing still images, providing flexibility for different creative workflows.
* **Photorealistic Human Generation:** A significant improvement over previous models, Gen-3 Alpha can generate expressive, lifelike human characters with realistic actions, gestures, and emotions.

## Example Use Cases

* **Filmmaking and Storyboarding:** Pre-visualizing scenes, generating b-roll footage, or creating entire short films with specific camera directions.
* **Advertising and Marketing:** Rapidly prototyping video ads or creating highly stylized promotional content without expensive physical shoots.
* **Visual Effects:** Generating specific elements (like explosions, weather effects, or abstract animations) to composite into existing footage.

## Intended Use & Limitations

* **Duration Limits:** Currently, the model is optimized for generating short clips (typically 5 to 10 seconds), though techniques exist to extend or loop these generations.
* **Complex Physics:** While improved, the model may still occasionally struggle with highly complex physical interactions or morphing objects unexpectedly.
