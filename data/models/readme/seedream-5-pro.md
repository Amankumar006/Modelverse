## Model Overview
Seedream 5 Pro is a flagship AI image generation and editing model released by ByteDance on July 1, 2026. Positioned as a professional-grade tool for complex visual communication, Seedream 5 Pro focuses heavily on tasks where layout, typography, and precise editing are critical. Representing a significant evolution in ByteDance's "Seed" series, the model natively generates and breaks down transparent image layers, making it highly suitable for graphic design. It acts less like a simple text-to-image generator and more like a tool for "creative specification," interpreting prompts as comprehensive design briefs.

## Capabilities
* **Precision Editing & Layering:** Seedream 5 Pro allows for "grounded" edits, enabling users to target and change specific regions of an image while preserving the rest. It natively generates transparent layers, allowing elements to be separated and modified individually.
* **Sketch-to-Design:** The model translates rough user sketches and bounding box annotations into finalized, polished designs and UI mockups.
* **Native Multilingual Text Rendering:** It boasts strong multilingual text capabilities, capable of rendering native, readable text in 14 languages (including English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish) directly into images.
* **Multi-Reference Support:** It can process up to 10 reference images simultaneously to precisely guide the generation process for style, composition, or subject matter.
* **High-Resolution Output:** The model supports high-resolution outputs natively up to 2K.

## Example Use Cases
* **Complex Infographics:** Generating structured layouts with clear informational hierarchies and embedded text.
* **UI/UX Prototyping:** Turning rough wireframes, sketches, and detailed annotations into high-fidelity user interface mockups.
* **Professional Typography & Posters:** Designing visually striking posters and marketing graphics with accurate multilingual text and layered compositions.
* **Advanced Image Editing:** Utilizing sketch, anchor, and layer editing to meticulously refine and iterate on visual content within professional design workflows.

## Performance & Benchmarks
Seedream 5 Pro competes directly with other flagship proprietary models in the professional design space. Its ability to process multiple image references and perform precise text rendering places it at the forefront of models tailored for visual communication and creative specification, rather than just casual artistic generation.

## Intended Use & Limitations
**Intended Use:** The model is built for professional designers, UI/UX creators, and marketers who need precise structural editing, multi-layer generation, and accurate text rendering. It is available via API on the BytePlus ARK platform and integrated into tools like Krea.
**Limitations:** Seedream 5 Pro is a closed-source, API-only model, requiring an internet connection and subscription for access. Its focus on structured design briefs means it may be over-engineered for simple, casual image requests where multi-layer breakdowns are unnecessary.

## About ByteDance
ByteDance is a global technology company known for operating a range of popular content platforms, including TikTok and Douyin. Their AI research division has consistently pushed the boundaries of generative AI, particularly in video and image generation through their Seed and Dreamina (Jimeng) series of foundation models.
