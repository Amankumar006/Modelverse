# Seedream 5 Pro: Interactive Multimodal Image Generation & Editing

## Model Overview
**Seedream 5.0 Pro** is ByteDance's flagship multimodal image generation and interactive precision editing model, developed by the **ByteDance Seed research team** (released July 8, 2026).

Designed specifically for professional creative and enterprise production workflows (graphic design, marketing, storyboarding, e-commerce), Seedream 5.0 Pro moves beyond single-shot text-to-image generation to offer a native layer-based architecture, interactive grounded image manipulation, high-density multilingual typography, and multi-reference consistency up to 4K resolution.

---

## Key Features
- **Native Layered & Interactive Local Editing:** Supports precise local edits via point selection, lasso selection, sketch guidance, and color/material swapping without altering unselected regions.
- **High-Density Multilingual Typography:** Advanced layout and text rendering capabilities optimized for infographics and complex graphic designs across 10-14 languages.
- **Multi-Reference Consistency Control:** Accepts up to 10 reference images simultaneously to maintain strict character, object, and stylistic consistency.
- **High-Resolution Photorealistic Rendering:** Produces outputs up to 4K resolution with lifelike lighting, realistic skin textures, and CG-level expressiveness.
- **API & Ecosystem Integration:** Integrated into ByteDance's BytePlus ModelArk API and compatible with Seedance video generation pipelines.
