## Model Overview
GPT-5 (`gpt-5`) is an intelligent reasoning model from OpenAI, officially released on August 7, 2025. It was designed as a unified system replacing previous models like GPT-4o, and offering advanced reasoning, multimodal inputs (text, voice, vision), and task execution. As of July 2026, GPT-5 is no longer the latest frontier model, having been succeeded by the GPT-5.6 series (Sol, Terra, and Luna). 

## Capabilities
- **Advanced Reasoning**: Designed for complex coding, scientific analysis, and agentic tasks.
- **Real-Time Router**: Automatically manages query complexity by switching between "Fast" modes for routine tasks and "Thinking" modes for complex, multi-step reasoning.
- **Configurable Intelligence**: Features distinct cost tiers including GPT-5 mini (for cost-sensitive workloads), GPT-5 nano (fastest and most cost-efficient), and GPT-5 Pro (for maximum precision and intelligence).

## Example Use Cases
- **Agentic Workflows**: Autonomous task execution requiring deep reasoning and planning.
- **Software Engineering**: Advanced code generation, debugging, and architectural design.
- **Scientific Research**: Analyzing complex data and synthesizing information for professional use cases.

## Performance & Benchmarks
At its launch, GPT-5 represented a significant leap over the GPT-4 family. While specific benchmark details (like exact parameter count and context window) are not publicly detailed in its metadata, the model was renowned for its adaptive reasoning capabilities.

## Intended Use & Limitations
- **Intended Use**: Targeted at professional, agentic work where deep reasoning and high intelligence are required.
- **Limitations**: It is no longer the state-of-the-art model; users seeking the most current capabilities for frontier-level performance are now directed by OpenAI to the GPT-5.6 series.
