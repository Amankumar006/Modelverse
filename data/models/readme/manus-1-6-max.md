# Manus 1.6 Max

> **Note:** As of late 2025, reports indicated that the developer, Butterfly Effect, was acquired by Meta. Manus 1.6 Max represents the flagship tier of their autonomous agent ecosystem.

## Model Overview
**Manus 1.6 Max** is the flagship autonomous AI agent and action engine developed by Butterfly Effect. Unlike traditional conversational models that simply output text, Manus is designed as a "general AI agent" meant to perform actual work. Operating via a cloud-based, multi-agent architecture, it takes high-level user goals and autonomously plans, researches, and executes multi-step tasks across system-level virtualization and cloud sandboxes. The "Max" designation indicates the most powerful tier within the Manus 1.6 ecosystem (released alongside a "Lite" version), optimized specifically for deep research, complex reasoning, and end-to-end task execution requiring high persistence.

## Capabilities
*   **Autonomous Action Engine:** Manus can manage authenticated sessions, browse the web, operate cloud sandboxes, and perform full-stack code execution without manual human intervention.
*   **Wide Parallel Research:** Utilizes a system-level search engine that deploys parallel sub-agents to conduct deep, comprehensive research on complex topics.
*   **End-to-End Development:** Capable of writing, compiling, and deploying fully functional mobile applications and websites.
*   **Visual Chain-of-Thought & Design:** Includes a "Design View" for interactive, conversational image generation and modification, alongside advanced data analysis for creating boardroom-quality slide decks and financial dashboards.
*   **Asynchronous Operation:** Can continue working in the background after the user closes the interface, delivering the final output when the task is complete.

## Example Use Cases
*   **Automated Software Engineering:** Asking the agent to "build a mobile app for tracking daily expenses," which Manus will then design, code, and deploy autonomously.
*   **Deep Market Research:** Tasking the agent with generating a comprehensive market analysis report, including data extraction, financial modeling, and slide deck generation.
*   **Data Analysis & Visualization:** Converting raw financial or operational data into polished, interactive dashboards and visual presentations.

## Performance & Benchmarks
Exact parameter counts and context window sizes are currently undisclosed ("Proprietary"). However, Manus 1.6 Max is positioned as a highly persistent, high-compute model that prioritizes task completion rate and complex reasoning over the speed optimizations seen in its "Lite" counterpart. 

## Intended Use & Limitations
*   **Intended Use:** Aimed at enterprise users, developers, and knowledge workers who need to delegate complex, multi-step digital workflows to an AI that can act independently.
*   **Limitations:** Because it operates as an autonomous agent executing code and browsing the web, it may require careful oversight regarding the actions it takes on behalf of a user. The "Max" version may also have higher latency compared to conversational chatbots, as it is performing real-time research and multi-step planning.

## About Butterfly Effect
**Butterfly Effect** is a highly innovative AI startup, originally founded in China before relocating its headquarters to Singapore. Known also for the AI assistant Monica, the company made waves in the AI space by shifting the paradigm from "chatting" to "doing" with the launch of Manus in 2025. In late 2025, reports surfaced that Meta acquired Butterfly Effect, integrating its powerful agentic capabilities into broader Meta ecosystems, such as Meta Ads Manager.
