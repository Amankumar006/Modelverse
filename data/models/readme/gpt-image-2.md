# GPT Image 2

## Model Overview
**GPT Image 2** (`gpt-image-2`) is OpenAI’s state-of-the-art image generation model, officially launched on April 21, 2026. Serving as the flagship successor to GPT Image 1.5, it brings massive leaps in photorealism, text rendering, and generation speed. Built with a highly efficient new architecture, GPT Image 2 is natively multimodal and seamlessly integrated into the OpenAI ecosystem, offering unparalleled visual quality and control for professional workflows.

## Capabilities
* **Ultra-High-Fidelity Text Rendering:** Achieves ~99% accuracy in rendering text within images, supporting complex typography and multiple languages flawlessly.
* **Studio-Grade Photorealism:** Eliminates the common "AI look," offering cinematic lighting, superior color science, and highly realistic textures (such as human skin and fabrics).
* **Unprecedented Speed:** Generates images 3 to 5 times faster than its immediate predecessors, enabling near real-time multimodal interactions.
* **Flexible Aspect Ratios & High-Fidelity Inputs:** Supports a wide range of aspect ratios natively and can process high-resolution image inputs for precise style matching and targeted editing.
* **Native Conversational Integration:** Fully compatible with both the standard Images API and the multi-turn Responses API for fluid, conversational editing.

## Example Use Cases
* **Professional Product Photography:** Generating highly realistic product mockups with cinematic lighting and perfectly integrated brand text.
* **UI/UX Design Mockups:** Creating detailed and accurate user interface concepts with crisp, legible typography and modern aesthetics.
* **Consistent Character Design:** Producing characters with exact facial consistency and lifelike textures across multiple frames and generation cycles.
* **Real-Time Conversational Workflows:** Iterating on visual concepts rapidly within AI chat interfaces due to its extremely low latency architecture.

## Performance & Benchmarks
GPT Image 2 represents a major architectural leap, running 3–5x faster than GPT Image 1.5. Its near-perfect (~99%) text rendering accuracy and significant improvements in photorealism set a new industry benchmark. It largely solves the persistent issues of visual artifacting and "plastic" skin textures seen in older generative models.

## Intended Use & Limitations
GPT Image 2 is intended for both individual creators and enterprise developers requiring top-tier image generation. It is deployed as an API-only model for developers and is also natively integrated into consumer interfaces. Users must continue to adhere to OpenAI's strict safety and usage policies, particularly regarding the generation of photorealistic human likenesses and copyrighted materials.
