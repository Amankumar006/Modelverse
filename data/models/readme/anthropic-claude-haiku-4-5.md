## Model Overview
Claude Haiku 4.5 is a highly efficient generation 4 model developed by Anthropic, originally released on October 15, 2025. Designed as the fastest and most cost-effective model in its generation, it is aimed at high-volume, latency-sensitive applications. While highly capable upon release, as of mid-2026 it is considered a legacy model compared to the newer Claude 5 series.

## Capabilities
- **Extreme Speed and Efficiency:** Optimized for extremely low latency and high-volume automation.
- **Extended Thinking:** Introduced the ability to "reason" before responding.
- **Computer Use:** Built-in ability to interact with computer interfaces natively.
- **Context Handling:** A 200K token context window capable of understanding text and image modalities.

## Example Use Cases
- Real-time customer service agents and conversational AI.
- High-volume automation and lightweight corporate tasks.
- Multi-agent systems requiring fast and cost-effective inter-agent communication.

## Performance & Benchmarks
Upon release, Anthropic positioned Claude Haiku 4.5 as delivering performance comparable to earlier "Sonnet" models but at a significantly lower cost and higher speed. Today, it is largely viewed as a legacy option, with modern recommendations pointing to the 5-series models for state-of-the-art production deployments.

## Intended Use & Limitations
Claude Haiku 4.5 is best suited for applications where speed and cost are the primary factors. Due to its position as a generation 4 model, its reasoning capabilities are not as deep or sustained as the newer Claude 5 series (like Fable 5 or Mythos 5). It remains an API-only offering.

## About Anthropic
Anthropic is an AI research company focused on building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. They are the creators of the Claude family of models, emphasizing safety and alignment while pushing the boundaries of AI capabilities.
