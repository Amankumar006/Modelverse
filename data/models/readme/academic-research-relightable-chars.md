## ✨ Key Features

- Sparse-View Dynamic Relighting: Synthesizes free-view renders and relights dynamic human performances from sparse RGB videos
- Single-Pass RelightNet Architecture: Evaluates target environment lighting and complex light transport in a single feed-forward pass
- Physics-Informed 3D Gaussian Splatting: Combines 3D Gaussian splats attached to dynamic mesh proxies with physical features
- Alternating Illumination Capture: Multi-view lightstage protocol alternating uniform tracking frames with random environment maps
- Superior Generalization & Quality: Outperforms motion-driven avatar baselines in shadow rendering and identity preservation
