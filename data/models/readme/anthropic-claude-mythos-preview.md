## Model Overview
Due to its exceptional advanced capabilities, particularly in identifying software vulnerabilities, Anthropic chose not to release it to the general public. Instead, it was launched under "Project Glasswing", a defensive coalition involving major technology partners like AWS, Apple, Google, and Microsoft.## Capabilities
* **Advanced Cybersecurity:** The model possesses exceptional abilities to identify and exploit software vulnerabilities, often surpassing human experts, and has found thousands of high-severity, zero-day vulnerabilities in major operating systems and web browsers.
* **Complex Reasoning:** It showcases frontier-level complex reasoning and technical analysis.
* **Multimodal Processing:** Supports text, image, and code inputs with a massive context window of up to 1M tokens.

## Example Use Cases
* **Defensive Security Research:** Identifying and patching critical flaws in global digital infrastructure and software (under Project Glasswing).
* **Life Sciences Research:** Advanced biological and healthcare research within highly trusted, restricted access programs.
* **Vulnerability Auditing:** Conducting deep-dive audits on large codebases to discover zero-day exploits before malicious actors do.

## Intended Use & Limitations
**Intended Use:** 
This model is strictly intended for defensive cybersecurity research, advanced life sciences research, and enterprise safety testing by vetted partners. It is part of a controlled initiative to strengthen global digital infrastructure.

**Limitations & Restrictions:**
* **Not Publicly Available:** The model is highly restricted to vetted partners through Project Glasswing.
* **Safety Concerns:** Due to its powerful capabilities, there are significant risks of the model being used by malicious actors, which necessitated the restricted release strategy. (Note: A safer, public-facing counterpart with robust safety classifiers was later released as Claude Fable 5).
