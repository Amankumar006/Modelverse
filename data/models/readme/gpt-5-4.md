## Model Overview

**GPT-5.4** (`gpt-5-4`) is a major frontier model release from OpenAI, officially launched on March 5, 2026. Initially framed as a more affordable yet highly capable model for coding and professional work, the GPT-5.4 family introduced profound advancements in reasoning, deep web research, and agentic workflows.

The model family includes multiple tiers to balance cost and performance:
- **GPT-5.4 Pro:** The smartest version for precise and complex reasoning.
- **GPT-5.4 mini:** A highly capable, cost-efficient model for coding, computer use, and subagents.
- **GPT-5.4 nano:** The cheapest tier intended for simple, high-volume tasks.
- **GPT-5.4 Thinking:** A variant specifically designed to output an upfront "plan" of its reasoning process.

*Note: As of July 2026, GPT-5.4 is largely considered a legacy generation, having been succeeded by the GPT-5.5 series and the flagship GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, and Luna).*

## Capabilities

GPT-5.4 brought a paradigm shift to AI capabilities, featuring:
- **Native Computer-Use:** The ability to natively interact with desktop environments, manage spreadsheets, and control applications.
- **Advanced Reasoning:** The ability (specifically in the *Thinking* variant) to formulate and output a deliberate reasoning plan before executing an answer.
- **Massive Context:** A 1-million-token context window allowing for the ingestion of entire codebases, massive document libraries, or lengthy conversation histories.
- **Robust Tool-Use:** Deep integrations for web research and calling external APIs seamlessly.

## Example Use Cases

- **Agentic Computer Interaction:** Powering AI assistants that can visually and programmatically control a user's computer to complete workflow tasks like data entry or email management.
- **Deep Document Analysis:** Utilizing the 1-million-token context window to analyze hundreds of legal documents, financial reports, or entire software repositories at once.
- **Complex Software Development:** Using *GPT-5.4 mini* or *Pro* in subagent architectures to plan, write, and test software applications autonomously.

## Performance & Benchmarks

At its launch, GPT-5.4 established new state-of-the-art records across major LLM benchmarks for reasoning, coding, and tool use. Its massive context window showed near-perfect retrieval accuracy, and its computer-use capabilities marked a significant step forward in autonomous agent evaluations compared to GPT-5.3 iterations.

## Intended Use & Limitations

- **Intended Use:** General-purpose professional work, high-volume automated tasks (via the nano tier), and complex autonomous agent workflows (via the Pro and mini tiers). It is accessible as an API-only service.
- **Limitations:** While highly capable, it is now a legacy model. Users requiring the absolute highest reasoning capabilities or more modern modalities are encouraged to use the GPT-5.6 flagship series. Additionally, its native computer-use functionality requires strict sandboxing to prevent unintended system modifications.
