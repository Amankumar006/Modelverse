# GLM 5.2: Zhipu AI's Experimental Research Preview

## Model Overview
**GLM 5.2** is an experimental research preview model from Zhipu AI, released in July 2026 as a successor to the GLM 4.5 series. It represents Zhipu AI's continued research into next-generation language model architectures and capabilities. As a research preview, GLM 5.2 showcases novel methods and improvements over its predecessors while specific technical details remain under active development and disclosure.
