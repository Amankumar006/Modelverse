# Muscriptor: High-Fidelity Audio to MIDI Transcription

Muscriptor by Mirelo AI is an end-to-end neural audio transcription model designed to convert complex polyphonic music recordings into precise multi-track MIDI notation.

---

## 🎶 Capabilities & Performance

- **Multi-Instrument Polyphony**: Transcribes simultaneous piano, guitar, bass, and vocal tracks into separate MIDI channels.
- **Expressive Dynamics**: Captures note velocities, pitch bends, vibrato, and sustain pedal events.
- **Low Latency**: Processes 3-minute audio tracks in under 5 seconds.
